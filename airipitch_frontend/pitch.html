Capstone pitch · yellow taxi trips, January–June 2024

JFK to Manhattan takes 30 minutes at 4am and 68 at 7am.

We turned 20 million New York taxi trips into the answer to one question: when should you leave?

The problem

Traffic apps show the road right now. They don't show what a trip usually costs in time at each hour, which is what you need to plan ahead. New York publishes every taxi trip, but 20 million rows is too many to read by hand.

What we built

A pipeline that cleans the city's trip records and measures how taxis move between 263 zones, hour by hour, with a website where anyone can explore the results.

Why it's fast

The whole analysis runs on a graphics processor in 36 seconds. The same work on a regular processor takes over 5 minutes. That gap is what keeps it practical as new months, years and other ride services are added.

Who it's for

  • Travellers timing an airport run
  • Drivers choosing when and where to work
  • City planners finding the worst hours and zones

What it is not

It is not live traffic and it does not pick streets. Records show the zone a trip started and ended in, published about two months later. It covers yellow taxis only, and it describes the past, not a forecast.

What comes next

A trip-time lookup on the site: choose two zones and an hour. Then 2025 data, to measure what the congestion charge changed. A first look shows taxis inside the zone about 3% faster.
